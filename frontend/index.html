FoundIT: lost and found for Nile University and Baze University

FoundIT helps students, lecturers and staff at Nile University and Baze University get lost belongings back. Each school has its own private section. Report something you lost, post something you found with a photo, and the owner is told. Finders earn tokens when a return is confirmed, and owners can say thank you with a reward that is agreed privately in the chat.

  • A gallery of found items and a board of lost items, with notifications
  • A private chat between the owner and the finder
  • QR "Return me" tags you can stick on your laptop, bag or keys
  • Free to use with your school email

Log in or create an account to report or find an item.
